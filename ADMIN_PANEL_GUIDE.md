This guide explains what each menu option and feature in the Veenolex Admin Panel does. It is written in simple, clear language to help store managers and platform administrators run the daily quick-commerce operations.

## 1. Dashboard
The **Dashboard** is the main homepage screen that opens when you log into the Admin Panel. It gives you a quick snapshot of how your business is performing in real-time.
* **Key Stats:** View total sales revenue, active orders, new registrations, and completed deliveries.
* **Charts & Analytics:** Interactive graphs showing daily/weekly sales trends, peak ordering hours, and top-selling product categories.
* **Use this to:** Check your daily business health and see how many orders are flowing through the system.

---

## 2. Categories
Categories help customers find products easily on the app. The admin controls the categories structure up to 3 levels:
* **All Categories (Hierarchy):** Shows a tree view of how all your categories connect (e.g., *Grocery -> Fruits & Vegetables -> Apples*).
* **Header Categories (Level 1):** The primary categories shown at the top of the app homepage (e.g., *Grocery, Dairy, Electronics, Snacks*).
* **Main Categories (Level 2):** Sub-sections inside header categories (e.g., under *Grocery*, you have *Fresh Produce* and *Spices*).
* **Sub-Categories (Level 3):** The final classification where products are actually placed (e.g., *Green Vegetables*).
* **Use this to:** Add new category tags, upload category icons, and organize where products appear on the buyer app.

---

## 3. Products
This page lists all items uploaded to the platform by your registered sellers.
* **Product Review & Approval:** When a seller uploads a new product, it stays here. You can review the details, images, pricing, and approve or reject it before it goes live.
* **Search & Edit:** Search for any product by name, seller, or category, and edit its stock, price, description, or visibility status.
* **Use this to:** Keep product quality high and make sure no inappropriate or incorrectly priced items are listed on the app.

---

## 4. Marketing Tools
A set of features designed to help you run advertisements, send notifications, and configure discounts to attract more shoppers.
* **Create Sections (Experience Studio):** Customize the buyer app homepage. Drag-and-drop or edit banners, slider images, and promotion blocks.
* **Hero & Categories per page:** Choose which categories and hero banners appear first on specific search or collection landing pages.
* **Send Notifications:** Compose custom push notifications (with title, message body, and images) and send them directly to customers' mobile phones to announce sales or updates.
* **Coupons & Promos:** Create discount codes (e.g., `WELCOME10`, `FREEFEST`). You can set flat discounts, percentage-off discounts, validity dates, minimum order values, and limit usage per user.
* **Offer Sections:** Group discounted products under specific titles on the homepage (e.g., *"Deals under $99"* or *"Festive Offers"*).
* **Shop by Store:** Highlight selected stores or top-rated merchant banners on the app homepage.

---

## 5. Customer Support
Help customers resolve their queries and moderate user-generated content.
* **Help Tickets:** A real-time chat interface where customers can report delivery issues, payments, or item quality. You can chat with them directly and mark tickets as solved.
* **Review Content (Moderation):** Read reviews and ratings left by customers. You can remove spam, false reviews, or abusive comments to keep the ratings fair.

---

## 6. Sellers (Merchants)
Allows you to manage shop owners and businesses selling products on Veenolex.
* **Active Sellers:** A complete list of all currently active sellers. You can view their total sales, profile details, and linked products.
* **Waiting for Review (Pending):** When a new store registers, their application appears here. Review their business licenses, IDs, bank details, and approve or reject their registration.
* **Seller Locations:** A map visualizer showing exactly where all physical stores are located to help coordinate nearby deliveries.

---

## 7. Delivery Drivers
Manage your delivery fleet and ensure smooth order handovers.
* **Active Drivers:** List of registered delivery boys, showing their status (Online/Offline), vehicle registration details, and active order counts.
* **Waiting for Review (Pending):** Approve or reject driver applications after verifying their identity cards, driving licenses, and vehicle certificates.
* **Track Drivers (Fleet Tracking):** A live map dashboard displaying the GPS location of all online delivery drivers, indicating who is currently delivering an order.
* **Send Money:** Add credits or pay delivery incentives/fuel allowances directly to the driver's platform wallet.

---

## 8. Wallets & Financials
Keep track of all the incoming and outgoing money on the platform.
* **Wallet:** Shows the main admin account balances, platform commission earned from orders, and overall financial statements.
* **Money Requests (Withdrawals):** When sellers or drivers request to transfer their platform earnings to their bank accounts, their requests arrive here. The admin reviews and marks them as Paid after transfer.
* **Seller Payments:** View the detailed history of all payments and commissions split between the platform and individual sellers.
* **Collect Cash:** Tracks cash collected by delivery drivers for Cash on Delivery (COD) orders. When a driver returns to the hub and hands over the physical cash, the admin updates the status here to clear their balance.

---

## 9. Customers
A database of all registered customers on the platform.
* **Customer Detail:** Search for any customer to see their active orders, past order history, registered delivery addresses, and customer support history.
* **Actions:** Block users who violate terms or suspend accounts showing fraudulent behavior.

---

## 10. FAQs
Manage the Help Center contents.
* **Use this to:** Write, edit, and organize Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) (e.g., *"How do I refund my order?"*, *"What are the delivery hours?"*) that customers read on the mobile app.

---

## 11. Orders & Returns
The central tracking console for all customer orders.
* **Order Status Categories:**
  - **All Orders:** Complete history of every order placed.
  - **New Orders:** Orders recently placed that are waiting to be accepted.
  - **Being Prepared:** Accepted orders currently being packaged or cooked by the seller.
  - **On the Way:** Orders picked up by a delivery driver and en route to the customer.
  - **Delivered / Cancelled / Returned:** Final states of completed, rejected, or returned orders.
* **Return Requests:** Manage requests from customers wanting to return items. View images of the items, check the reasons, and approve or decline the refund.

---

## 12. Fees & Settings
Configuration settings that change how the app behaves.
* **Fees & Charges:** Modify delivery fees (flat-rate or distance-based), platform commissions (percentage taken from sellers per sale), taxes, and extra surge charges (e.g., for rain or late nights).
* **Settings:** General settings including shop operating hours, customer support contact info, app version checks, maintenance mode toggle, and developer environment details.
